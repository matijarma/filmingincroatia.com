how i would use this app

Estimates made here are for exploratory and ball-park purposes; it's meant to show you quickly and unambigously if your project can realistically expect financing support and to which degree so you can easily see if embarking on such a path is worth your time or not.

what happens after the first pass

Conventional wisdom dictates to check the 4 minority coproduction deadlines on havc.hr and establish contact with a Croatian producer.
I stand at your disposal for any consult on that matter over e-mail.

Estimator assumes general European co-production realities such as the European convention on coproductions to which Croatia is the signatory of and material differences between co-productions and service productions.
These qualifications include the cultural viability tests, investment reciprocity, insurance standards, fees&escrow procedures etc.

what i can review with you

I can review your package as a producer, help shape a credible financing path, recommend a list of viable producers and flag where the assumptions are too optimistic before you lose time in formal applications.

A live estimator built on the current Croatian rules. The hard numbers come from Narodne novine; the soft ones (HAVC award sizing, local top-ups) come from producer practice and are labelled as such inside the constellation.

incentive rebate

25% of qualifying Croatian spend, plus 5% if the production films in a below-average-development region. Eligible spend is capped at 80% of total budget. Minimum Croatian spend: €250,000 for a feature, €60,000 for a documentary. Shorts are not rebate-eligible.

havc minority co-production

Available to projects with 50–85% secured financing. At least 60% of any HAVC grant must be spent in Croatia. Award amounts are discretionary (HAVC's Umjetničko vijeće decides per project) — the band shown in the constellation is our practitioner estimate calibrated against 2024–2025 averages.

local / municipal funds

City and county culture funds (Zagreb, Istria, Rijeka, Split-Dalmatia, Pula, …) typically top up once HAVC's minority approval is in hand — the domino effect. Amounts vary; €10–25k is a common first-domino range.

eligibility math

To apply for the rebate you need ≥ 70% of your Croatian spend secured — not your total budget, since HAVC has no jurisdiction over non-Croatian budget. To be an official minority co-production you need ≥ 15% Croatian share, which is why anything above ~85% secured is essentially out of scope for HAVC minority funding.

what this calculator is

A live estimator for first-pass financing conversations. Real decisions sit with HAVC, the line producer, and the auditor. The constellation labels each source as indicative where the calc is using a practitioner heuristic rather than a codified rule.
